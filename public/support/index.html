Slop Support

Questions, account help, bug reports, and safety concerns

Need help with the Slop app, your account, or a game? Email support@slop.game and our team will help you.

Get faster help

Include your Slop username or account email, device type, app version, what you expected to happen, and the steps that caused the problem. Screenshots are useful when available. Never send your password or an API key.

Safety reports

Use the in-app reporting tools for games, messages, and creators whenever possible. For urgent safety concerns, contact safety@slop.game.

Privacy and legal

Privacy requests: privacy@slop.game
Copyright notices: copyright@slop.game
Legal questions: legal@slop.game
