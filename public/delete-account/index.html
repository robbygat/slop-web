Your account

Delete your Slop account

Request account and associated-data deletion, even if you no longer have the app.

In the app

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account. You may need to sign in again to confirm that the account is yours.

Without the app

Email privacy@slop.game with the subject “Slop.game account deletion request.” Include the email address or username for the account you want deleted. Do not send your password or an API key. We may reply to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

What happens next

We will process a verified request to delete your account and associated personal data, and confirm when it is complete. Some records may be retained where needed for security or legal obligations, as described in our Privacy Policy. For questions, contact privacy@slop.game.
