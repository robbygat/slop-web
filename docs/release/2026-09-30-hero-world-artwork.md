## Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background image for the right half of the Slop.game website hero, behind a live 3D cast of chrome toy robots (robots will be added in code; do NOT draw any robots). Create one highly polished landscape image, 3:2 aspect ratio, a playful floating arcade dreamscape in clean dimensional 3D illustration. An airy pale mint and butter-cream sky, soft lavender distant cloud forms, a few elegantly sculpted floating lime/seafoam islands at the bottom edges, one coral-orange geometric arch far on the right, tiny distant translucent blue glass shapes. It should feel like a tactile, art-directed game world, cool and inviting, with real depth and restrained detail. The central 65 percent must be calm open atmospheric space so seven animated robot heads can read clearly in front. Keep scenery mostly at the outer edges and bottom with a light horizon; no foreground objects covering the center. Use clean broad color fields, soft ambient occlusion and warm highlights, premium playful chrome-robot brand aesthetic. Must also crop beautifully to a square for a mobile hero without losing the calm central space. No text, no logos, no characters, no screens, no UI, no rings or orbital ellipses, no line swirls or gray squiggles, no grain/noisy texture, no watermark. Give the environment distinct mint, lime, lavender, pale sky-blue and coral color, avoid an all-white or gray scene.
